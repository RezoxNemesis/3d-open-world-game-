# Architecture and production backlog

## Current homes and hero powers — version 0.5

The main scene still uses one physics traveler and nine bounded terrain regions. `verdant_route.gd` adds a second real elevated route: thirty 0.20m risers, a covered bridge and a supported six-meter Fox Shrine terrace. `verdant_district.gd` furnishes the scholar hall and gardens. `residence_route.gd` loads an original two-room home at `(20,0.6,31)`, applies authored collision profiles to its floor, walls, posts, ceiling, furniture and roof, and connects its front entrance to the courtyard by a supported side path and three steps. A separate far shell avoids rendering interior furniture at long range. The collision JSON is explicitly included and byte-verified in the APK.

`architectural_detail.gd` discovers actual placed module transforms and attaches curved ceramic tiles, rafters, corbels and fixings through four-meter spatial MultiMeshes. Actual-world index-buffer accounting measures **2,061,008 additional highest-detail triangles**, including the home's near roof; the far house is excluded. This is a whole-world content count. High uses the richer tile shell within30m; Balanced uses a coarser curved shell within14m; Performance hides this layer while retaining base buildings. Counts never add both tile alternatives. Independent geometry and final rendered-pass measurements are recorded separately.

`architecture_collision.gd` gives the existing visible building roofs and opaque facades real collision for flight and roof landing. It uses 87,604 base-mesh triangles in 17 static bodies; decorative tile layers, cloth/gold and the already physical residence are excluded. Actual-player tests retain hall access and walking around visible furniture while rejecting ceiling and upper-wall pass-through.

`verdant_flora.gd` adds gnarled trees, wisteria, roots, flowers, moss and ground detail. Crown-aware exclusions protect the western route, shrine and home path. The revised ground garden has160,922 full-density triangles across145 spatial batches before quality culling; ledge vegetation is separate. `celestial_dragon.gd` follows a closed, bounded sky path using an original twenty-joint skinned animation and near/far geometry. This is ambient scenery; dragon riding is not implemented.

`world_activities.gd` manages three proximity-checked original records and a shrine bell that requires the completed lantern errand plus all three records. The earned jade seal is actual traveler geometry. `quest_interface.gd` displays nearby records, an earned-only scrolling journal and a completion-gated bell dialogue while preserving keeper/minimap behavior. The fifth discovery is the Fox Shrine.

`hero_powers.gd` composes velocity through `player.gd`'s existing single `move_and_slide()` call. Flight provides36m/s camera-directed travel,90m/s RUN boost,20m/s vertical controls, hover and a160m altitude cap. Held LEAP charges for up to1.1s and releases18–52m/s vertical velocity. SPEED provides72m/s ground movement and120m/s RUN boost, with a swept capsule and a smoothly restored camera FOV. Streaming prediction and periodic polling share the same desired origin to avoid rebuilding opposite chunk sets near boundaries.

`destructible_mountains.gd` creates three explicitly breakable18–22m karst formations, identified by stable IDs. SMASH removes their actual visible nodes and collision, with a three-second cooldown, bounded24-piece debris pool and short-lived shockwave. Loading an earlier save or pressing SETTINGS→RESTORE MOUNTAIN CRAGS restores the original formations. The settings action preserves quest rewards and hero modes, and the next save retains the restored state. Arbitrary terrain mining and general combat remain unimplemented.

`hero_power_controls.gd` uses independent native touch buttons with published real hit rectangles and charge feedback. Power fingers cannot become camera fingers. Deliberate LEAP release launches; focus loss, canceled touch, resize and modal transitions clear charge and native latches. The secondary DOWN target avoids the top-row MAP control even with a left safe-area inset. `mobile_controls.gd` retains its existing movement/look/manualRUN behavior and adds optional strictly validated edge-of-pad sprint assistance.

`save_store.gd` writes schema5: position, five discovery IDs, quest stage, activity state and `{flying,fast,destroyed}` hero state. Versions1–4 migrate without granting unearned progress. Type, duplicate-ID, coordinate and size guards precede atomic writes. `journey_checkpoint.gd` debounces Android milestones and retries failed writes at bounded intervals; desktop autosave defaults off. It keeps confirmed ground positions separately from flight samples so turning flight off cannot save an unsupported airborne checkpoint. Restore guards prevent loading from immediately overwriting the save.

## Historical Celestial District implementation — version 0.3

`scenes/main.tscn` composes the world, physics traveler and input/HUD coordinators. `scripts/world.gd` builds the sanctuary, side pavilions, eastern gate, lotus waterfront, routes, irregular mountain silhouettes and waterfalls. The original curved-roof Blender/glTF kit remains in use, supplemented by seven original celestial modules in `assets/models/celestial/`. The central sanctuary now has three architectural levels; its upper galleries are scenic. `scripts/architectural_palette.gd` shares named PBR materials across the new texture-free GLBs. The sanctuary plinth and separately aligned pillar collision preserve the traversable original ground floor and eight 0.22 m entrance risers. `scripts/celestial_district.gd` batches market canopies, guardians, planted containers, balustrades and ceremonial props around clear walking space.

`scripts/landscape_visuals.gd` connects the eastern gate `(30,0,-4)` to an approach deck at `(50,0,-4)`, then a 4 m clear stair lane with fifty 0.20 m risers and 0.55 m treads to the upper terrace at height 10 m. The terrace is centered near `(46,10,-39)` and has a notch around the final stair run so its supporting slab cannot block ascent. The pagoda, fourth landmark and jade lantern stand at `(46,10,-42)`. Stair-side guards and separately authored column collision retain the walking lane. Other distant cliff pavilions remain scenic rather than additional authored climbing routes.

`scripts/nature.gd` creates seeded forked blossom/maple trees, layered Chinese-pine sprays, rocks, grass and ferns in 48 m spatial MultiMesh groups. Original painted leaf/petal/needle cards replace rounded crown solids and hashed holes. Local-axis branch scaling preserves exact authored endpoints, including forward-parallel branches. Camera-only flowering-canopy colliders use layer 2 while the traveler uses layer 1. Routes, pools, the upper stair and terrace exclude generated ground plants. Deterministically shuffled leaf clusters let quality presets thin every tree instead of stripping whole crowns. `GardenNature` preserves same-seed ground placement; 32 smaller ledge pines live separately under `MountainWoodlands`, and both receive density/range settings. Original bark, botanical and limestone-runoff/moss maps supply material detail without reference-image extraction.

`scripts/region_streamer.gd` preserves nine 128 m regions. Each 32×32 grid has walkable collision and analytical smooth vertex normals; globally sampled height gives matching shared edges and deterministic regeneration. Loading a saved distant position builds its neighboring terrain before moving the player. Generation remains synchronous. The district and its vegetation remain a bounded authored scene rather than region-streamed content.

`scripts/player.gd` implements analog camera-relative movement, acceleration/braking, 5 m/s walking, 8 m/s held sprint, articulated walking/running, jumping, first/third person, damped camera targets, spring-arm collision and fall recovery. A bounded 0.28 m capsule-clearance step probe climbs authored stairs while preserving higher-wall and overhead obstruction checks. `scripts/traveler_visual.gd` generates shaped clothing, a face/hair silhouette, boots, equipment, articulated elbows/knees and moving coat tails, with a shared original woven cloth map. `scripts/mobile_controls.gd` maps a floating lower-left joystick with radial dead zone, independent actions and right-side camera swipe. Responsive safe-area controls and expanded stretch aspect use the wide phone view.

`scripts/exploration.gd` discovers Cloud Sanctuary, Lotus Overlook, Eastern Gate and Sky Temple by reaching them within horizontal and vertical bounds. The compact HUD reports real progress and distance; the full map permits travel only to discovered waypoints. `scripts/quest_interface.gd` adds a north-up schematic minimap with the actual route, landmarks, player heading and current quest target, plus proximity-dependent interaction and Keeper Sen's dialogue. `scripts/lantern_quest.gd` implements one complete errand: accept near Sen in the courtyard, recover the jade lantern on the upper terrace, return to Sen and earn a visible hip lantern. Stages 0/1/2/3 represent available, accepted, recovered and completed. Arbitrary distant UI presses do not advance it. Map, settings and dialogue pause physics and release actions, fingers and native-button latches; focus loss also clears movement velocity.

`save_store.gd` writes version 3 position, discovered landmark IDs and quest stage with strict type/schema/size/coordinate checks and atomic write-error handling. Version 1 position saves and version 2 discovery saves migrate with quest stage 0. Invalid loads preserve the current journey, and loading a completed stage restores its earned cosmetic. `game_settings.gd` separately persists validated quality, sensitivity, pad size and camera distance. Full mutable world-state persistence remains future work.

`assets/shaders/` implements worn paving, cloud sky, moving botanical cards, animated jade water, detailed waterfalls, impact spray and silk banners using Compatibility features. Warmer sunlight, cool ambient light and nonvolumetric distance fog separate the district from its mountain backdrop. One sun casts bounded two-split shadows; two short-range non-shadow lights supplement emissive lantern art. Shoreline rocks/reeds, impact foam and a small shared billboard spray surround the pools and falls; spray is disabled in Performance mode. `scripts/ambience.gd` plays a quiet original 20-second Vorbis loop of synthesized wind/water, bird motifs and a bell. Audio clipping/looping/import were checked; listening remains unverified.

## Mobile target and content budgets

Realme P1 Speed, Android ARM64. Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility/OpenGL, 1280×720 base canvas expanded for wide landscape. Presets: Performance 0.70 rendering scale/no MSAA/no sun shadows/42% scalable foliage; Balanced 0.85 / 2×MSAA / 45 m shadows/72%; High 1.0 / 4×MSAA / 65 m shadows/full density. Android directional shadow map is 1024. FXAA was rejected because this engine's Compatibility renderer reports it unsupported; captures use supported MSAA. No sustained phone FPS/heat/memory claim is made from software desktop rendering or headless physics.

Terrain content: nine active regions, 18,432 grid triangles total. The original kit remains: hero pavilion 19,068 triangles/six materials; distant pavilion 2,516/six; gate 17,468/five; lantern 564/six; bridge 1,432/four; shrine 7,436/five. The new manifest records sanctuary 35,244/six, pagoda 18,548/five, market stall 876/five, guardian 1,010/two, planter 712/three, balustrade 396/three and incense burner 1,180/two. All new GLBs share runtime materials. Repeated props use spatial batching; tiny props, lantern art and distant pavilions omit shadows.

The version 0.5 ground garden contains160,922 triangles across145 spatial batches at full density, plus separate ledge vegetation. The historical version 0.3 garden had169,868 triangles/103 batches and25,728 additional ledge-pine triangles. Flower/pine cards use alpha-scissor shadows, and camera collision stays separate from those visual surfaces. These are content counts, not rendered-pass or performance results. The current Balanced courtyard target remains below800 draw calls and700k total triangles across passes; source-matched evidence belongs in `docs/TEST_RESULTS.md` and `artifacts/district-render-metrics.json`. ## Version 0.6 expedition layer

The temple district keeps its previous quests, powers and version5 journey save. MAP opens a separate expedition scene; its coordinate save is `user://expedition.json`, and returning reloads the preserved temple journey. The expedition uses 128m integer sectors with local float coordinates in [-64,64): x spans exactly -100,000,000m to+100,000,000m. Nine surrounding sectors are resident at most; central collision loads synchronously, neighbor scenery is deferred by sector. The atlas provides long-distance routes. Generated regions repeat architectural modules; the large domain is not all unique authored content.

Sector(0,0) owns the specifically composed Lotus Cliff Sanctuary, water garden, covered crossing, reachable hillside pavilion and residents. All their nodes belong to the region and unload together. Origin serialization normalizes coordinates even between player physics and the following rebase. Both rider and vehicle respect the domain bounds; distant parked carts suspend gravity before their ground unloads.

The original guardian encounter uses telegraphed area strikes, facing/range/occlusion checked melee, swept-collision dodge, recoverable defeat and retry. Wind cart motion uses a real CharacterBody3D, conservative hull/mast clearance, grounded safe dismount, parked-state handling and fall recovery. Encounter and mount state reset on atlas travel/reload; the expedition save currently preserves coordinates, not vehicle posture or guardian progress.

Conservative real-render QA thresholds at1600x720 Balanced are500total draws and400,000 total-pass triangles for thirteen named expedition views. These are software renderer counters, not phone FPS. Existing district thresholds and million-triangle highest-detail architecture remain unchanged. Shared terrain grids reduce redundant vertex/derivative construction while retaining the same ground triangles and smooth seam normals.


## Stability and durable expedition progress — v0.6.1

The expedition journal stores the latest128 distinct visited regions and their calmed-guardian flags. Oldest records are evicted together at the bound. Save schema2 migrates schema1 coordinate-only saves; a checked parser, numeric version check and complete journal/coordinate validation reject malformed snapshots before altering live state. The journal is independent of the temple district's existing journey save. Atlas travel/reload restores a recorded peaceful guardian; RETRY is a practice encounter and does not erase prior completion. Walking sector transitions keep the existing encounter and vehicle lifecycle.

Authored collision resources are immutable and cached up to16 shapes across expedition instances. Rock mesh/collision and ground materials are shared. Terrain height sampling uses289 unique grid points per region, retaining identical triangles/normals/collision. No unbounded terrain cache is retained. Mobile control disposal releases analog input even when a scene is replaced directly.

The guardian's existing head and foreleg geometry now articulates through warning, strike, recovery and calm poses. A shared ring mesh provides a countdown and impact pulse. The timing remains dodgeable; victory signals include the encounter's original sector even after floating-origin movement. Rider defeat immediately stops the cart; mounted encounter disposal restores collision/control ownership.

The owner's GTA/Genshin benchmark means cohesive art, responsive locomotion/combat, meaningful activities, inhabitants and world interactions. The current procedural prototype remains well below those games. Next production slice: one polished sanctuary-to-village route with authored terrain/materials, humanoid animation, resident activity and a complete quest, profiled on the Android target before increasing world density. Increasing the coordinate span alone does not add authored content or visual quality.
