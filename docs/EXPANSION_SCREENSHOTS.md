# Temple Realm v0.7 — 27 in-game screenshots

[Download all 27 original screenshots](TempleRealm_v0.7_Screenshots.zip) · [Controls and features](REALMS_AND_POWERS.md)

These are real renders of the playable game from the cloud workspace, including staged camera views. They are not concept art or phone captures. The large map uses repeated procedural regions; this gallery shows the authored destinations and a distant region, not unique scenery across every kilometer. Visuals remain a stylized prototype.
